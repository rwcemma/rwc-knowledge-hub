The questions I get asked most

Why is it a duo?

Because a binder and an open filter are two different jobs, and either one alone leaves you half-covered.

  • BioToxin Binder catches what is circulating and carries it out.*
  • KL Support keeps the kidneys and liver — the route everything has to travel through — doing their job while you ask more of them.*

Take a binder with a sluggish liver and congested kidneys and you have caught the toxins but not moved them. Support the filter without a binder and you have opened the road with nothing loaded onto it. Together they open the exit and walk the load through it.

What is the protocol?

Start here, and give it at least two weeks before you begin any parasite or deeper detox protocol. Then keep it running through that protocol — drainage isn’t a phase you finish, it’s the thing that has to stay open the whole time.

  • Follow the dosing on each bottle — both are dosed on their own labels, not against each other.
  • Keep the binder away from food and other supplements by about 30 minutes. A binder taken alongside them will bind some of what you actually wanted absorbed.
  • Hydrate with minerals, sweat, and move your lymph daily. Binders without water is how people get stopped up.

Not moving your bowels at least once a day yet? Add Bowel Mover before anything else. A binder with no bowel movement has nowhere to take what it caught.

How do I know my drainage is actually open?

You don’t have to guess. There are four things I want to see before I’d clear anyone to start a cleanse:

  • Two full, relieving bowel movements a day. Not one, not “most days.”
  • You sweat — sauna, movement, hot bath. Something.
  • Urine is pale and frequent, not dark and scarce.
  • You’re not permanently puffy, and mornings don’t start with a headache.

If you’re there, you’re ready for the next step. If you’re not, this is exactly where you should be — and the fix is time on this duo, not a stronger product.

What if I’m sensitive?

This is the gentlest place to start in the whole line, which is precisely why I route reactive people here first. Nothing in it is designed to kill anything — it’s support, not attack.

That said, if you flare from most supplements, go slow and dose low: a half, a quarter, even an eighth of the label dose while you evaluate tolerance. And keep the bowels moving. Most bad reactions to a binder are a constipation problem, not a binder problem.

If that sensitivity is stacked on top of active mold, active Lyme or significant MCAS, please don’t work this out alone. Generic protocols in complex cases either work beautifully or backfire badly, and which one you get comes down to bio-individualized protocol design.
